## Why a separate notice

Open-source licenses like MIT intentionally say nothing about trademarks —
they're a different kind of right, and conflating the two would either force
every fork to rebrand (which MIT doesn't require) or let forks imply an
affiliation that doesn't exist (which trademark law doesn't allow). Keeping
them separate lets the code be as freely reusable as MIT promises, while the
name and logo stay tied to their actual source.
